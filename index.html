Pine Game Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 12, 2026
Developer: U1 Games (Google Play developer account "Startup Companys")
Contact: u1gamestudio@gmail.com

Summary

Pine Game is an offline puzzle game. It does not collect, store on our servers, or share any personal information.

Information we collect

We do not collect any personal or device information. The game does not require an account, does not ask for your name, email address, location, contacts, photos or any other personal data, and does not use the advertising ID.

Information stored on your device

To remember your progress, the game saves your current level and earned stars in the app's private storage on your own device. This data never leaves your device. It is removed when you clear the app's data or uninstall the game.

Internet, advertising and analytics

The current version of Pine Game does not connect to the internet, does not show ads and does not include analytics or third-party tracking tools. If a future version adds advertising or analytics, we will update this policy and the Data safety section on Google Play before that version is released.

Children

Pine Game is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date above shows when it last changed.

Contact

Questions about this policy: u1gamestudio@gmail.com
